Hello!

I'm a Product Manager who bridges the gap between cutting-edge AI technology and human-centered design, leveraging my background in Cognitive Science to create AI products that truly understand how people think and work.

Currently Product Manager at Phillip Nova, I oversee trading platform ecosystems for the Singapore and Malaysia markets, leading automated account opening, client portal enhancements and single sign-on integration, all within complex regulatory frameworks.

Journey to Product

It started with a BSc in Cognitive Sciences from UNIMAS, where I got fascinated by how humans process information, make decisions and learn. Concepts like cognitive load, mental models and attention directly shape how I design AI products and workflows.
